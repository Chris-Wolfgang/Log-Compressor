# ADR-NNNN: Short imperative title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [ADR-NNNN](NNNN-slug.md) | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What situation forces a decision? State the constraints (technical, product,
tooling) that make the choice non-obvious. Two or three paragraphs at most.

## Decision

The choice that was made, stated in the active voice ("We ship logc as ...").
Include the load-bearing details a future maintainer would otherwise have to
re-derive.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what follow-up work the decision
creates. List trade-offs honestly — the point of an ADR is to record what was
knowingly given up.
